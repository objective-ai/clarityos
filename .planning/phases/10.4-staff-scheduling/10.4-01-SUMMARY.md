## What Was Built

### ORM Models (backend/db/models/tenant/clinical.py)
- `BlockType` enum: LUNCH, HOLIDAY, PERSONAL, OTHER (str enum, 4 values)
- `StaffWeeklySchedule`: day_of_week (0-6), start_time/end_time (Time columns), is_active; UniqueConstraint(staff_id, day_of_week)
- `StaffBlockedTime`: start_datetime/end_datetime, reason, block_type String(20); composite index (staff_id, start_datetime)
- `StaffAttendance`: clock_in_at, clock_out_at (nullable), date, notes; composite index (staff_id, date)
- Staff back-references: weekly_schedules, blocked_times, attendance (all lazy="selectin", cascade="all, delete-orphan")

### Permissions (backend/core/permissions.py)
- `MANAGE_SCHEDULE` → roles: doctor, admin, owner
- `CLOCK_IN_OUT` → roles: doctor, technician, receptionist, admin, owner (all staff)
- `VIEW_ATTENDANCE` → roles: admin, owner

### Pydantic Schemas (backend/schemas/staff_schedule.py)
Exports: WeeklyScheduleDayRequest, WeeklyScheduleBulkRequest, WeeklyScheduleDayResponse, BlockedTimeRequest, BlockedTimeResponse, StaffAvailabilityEntry, WeeklyAvailabilityResponse, ClockInResponse, ClockOutResponse, ClockStatusResponse, AttendanceRecord, AttendanceSummary

- `WeeklyScheduleDayResponse` uses `@field_serializer("start_time", "end_time")` to serialize datetime.time as "HH:MM" strings
