## Strict Timezone Wiring

`_resolve_tenant_tz(db, tenant_id)` loads `Tenant` via `db.get(Tenant, tenant_id)` and reads `tenant.timezone` (direct column, `server_default="America/Los_Angeles"`). Raises HTTP 400 `"Tenant timezone not configured"` if tenant is missing or timezone is empty. Returns `ZoneInfo(tz_name)`.

`_clinic_date_from_utc(dt, tz)` converts UTC datetime to clinic-local date using the resolved `ZoneInfo`. No UTC fallback exists in either helper.

Both `clock_in` and `clock_out` call `_resolve_tenant_tz` before any DB write — the timezone check is symmetric.

**Resolved settings key:** `Tenant.timezone` (direct column, not `settings_jsonb["timezone"]`).
