## What Was Built

### BookingDrawer (`components/schedule/BookingDrawer.tsx`)

New right-side drawer (480px, z-50, slide from right) that replaces the modal dialog:

- **Slot picker grid** (`grid-cols-4`): generates 20 slots from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM in 30-min increments. Each slot computes ISO start/end, calls `isSlotOccupied()` to check availability.
- **Slot states**: available (clickable, accent hover), occupied (greyed, `cursor-not-allowed`, hover tooltip with patient name), selected (accent border/bg).
- **Conflict warning banner**: amber `bg-[#FBBF24]/10` banner with text "This slot already has an appointment. Proceeding will overbook the provider." — does not block submission.
- **Patient search**: debounced `apiFetch('/api/patients?search=...')` with dropdown results.
- **Inline new-patient form**: first name, last name, DOB, sex, phone — creates via POST `/api/patients`, auto-selects on success.
- **createAppointment** from store called on submit; refreshes appointments on success.
- ESC key + backdrop click close the drawer.

### Schedule Page (`app/(tenant)/[tenant]/schedule/page.tsx`)

- Replaced `BookAppointmentModal` import with `BookingDrawer`.
- `<BookingDrawer>` rendered using `drawer.mode === "booking"` state directly.
- `staffList` mapped inline at call site to `{ id, full_name }` format.
- Removed: `bookingOpen`, `bookingDefaults`, `closeDrawer`, `handleBook` (all now handled by drawer state machine).
