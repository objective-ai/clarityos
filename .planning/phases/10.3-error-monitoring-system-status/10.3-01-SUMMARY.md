## Decisions Made

See frontmatter `key-decisions`. Highlights:

- One combined deny list (snake + camel) rather than two sets — avoids a second pass and matches reality: PHI appears in both shapes depending on whether it leaked pre- or post-apiFetch.
- `path.startswith(prefix)` for clinical match so sub-routes (`/api/encounters/123/vitals`) are covered by parent prefix.
- Structural `SentryEvent` type in TS scrubber keeps this plan independent of `@sentry/nextjs` installation (which is Plan 10.3-02's responsibility).
