**Pitfalls:**
- Idempotency: the `Product.is_active.is_(True)` filter is critical — without it, a soft-deleted then re-added product would fail the partial unique index and raise `IntegrityError` on a re-run. The check matches the partial unique index from migration 0017.
- Use snake_case keys in JSONB `attributes` — see Pitfall 1 in 13-RESEARCH.md and the inventory store's raw-fetch opt-out (Wave 3).
- `Decimal(str(retail))` is intentional — `Decimal(195.00)` (float) introduces precision drift; `Decimal("195.00")` does not. Mirroring `_seed_insurance_payers`.
- The seed function MUST be sync (`Session`, not `AsyncSession`) — `backend/seed_db.py` is run as a one-shot script, not inside the FastAPI event loop.
- Total counts: exactly 10 frames + 5 contacts. The test stub `test_inventory_seed` will assert these counts.
