### Re-verification Notes

Truths 9 and 10 were initially reported as FAILED in the first verification pass. Investigation during gap closure planning found both were false negatives:

**Truth 9 (Schedule eligibility badges):** The initial verifier searched `schedule/page.tsx` directly for `SCHED_ELIG_DOT` and `insurance_eligibility` keywords. The rendering actually occurs in `components/schedule/AppointmentCard.tsx` (a child component imported at line 16 and rendered at line 368 of schedule/page.tsx). AppointmentCard.tsx has the SCHED_ELIG_DOT color map at line 19 and renders the eligibility badge at lines 183-187. This was committed in `9c8b655` before the initial verification.

**Truth 10 (TS Appointment type):** The initial verifier searched `types/appointment.ts` for snake_case field names (`insurance_payer_name`, `insurance_copay`, `insurance_eligibility`). The actual fields use camelCase (`insurancePayerName`, `insuranceCopay`, `insuranceEligibility`) at lines 57-59, matching the frontend convention. This was also committed in `9c8b655`.
