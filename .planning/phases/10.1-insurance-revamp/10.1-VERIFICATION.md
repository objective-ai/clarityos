### Gaps Summary

Two related gaps from Plan 03 Task 2 (schedule view + TS appointment type):

**Root cause:** The backend half of Plan 03 Task 2 was fully executed (appointment.py batch query, appointment.py schema fields), but the frontend half was skipped — neither the `schedule/page.tsx` eligibility badge rendering nor the `types/appointment.ts` type update were applied.

**Impact:** The schedule view cannot display eligibility badges even though the data is being returned by the API. TypeScript will likely produce type errors if the schedule page is ever updated to access `appt.insurance_eligibility` because the Appointment interface does not declare those fields.

**Scope:** Both gaps are in the same logical unit (frontend schedule view integration) and can be fixed together in one small plan targeting `app/(tenant)/[tenant]/schedule/page.tsx` and `types/appointment.ts`.
